About

I’m Santhosh Kumar, Managing Partner at PropFetch Realty and a RERA-certified real estate professional with over 10 years of experience in the real estate industry. My work is primarily focused on large land parcels, luxury real estate, and commercial leasing, where I help connect the right investors, developers, and businesses with high-value property opportunities. Over the years, I have built a strong network within the real estate ecosystem, working closely with landowners, developers, investors, and fellow brokers to structure and close meaningful transactions. My approach is deal-focused and relationship-driven, with a strong emphasis on transparency and long-term partnerships. Beyond real estate, I have also been involved in international mining asset transactions, which helped me build a global network of investors and asset owners. This exposure to cross-border deals has strengthened my ability to connect capital with strategic assets and facilitate complex opportunities. I’m always open to collaborating with brokers, developers, mandate holders, and investors working on premium and large-ticket opportunities. If you’re working on an interesting mandate or looking to structure a strategic deal, feel free to connect.
